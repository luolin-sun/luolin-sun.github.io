Luolin Sun

Postdoctoral Researcher, COAPS, Florida State University

I am a physical oceanographer with a background in applied mathematics. Combining theory with numerical ocean modelling, I study ocean dynamics and transport across scales, from mesoscale eddies and mean–eddy interactions to large-scale circulation.

My recent research focuses on Arctic Ocean warming and its connections with the subpolar North Atlantic through ocean transport. Looking ahead, I am also interested in how smaller-scale processes influence Arctic Ocean dynamics.

Happy to connect if you're interested in these topics!
